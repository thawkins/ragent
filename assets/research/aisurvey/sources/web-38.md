In remarks by the FSB Secretary General, the speaker argued that the financial system is changing rapidly amid Middle East and Ukraine conflicts, decade-high long-term bond yields, expanding and increasingly interconnected nonbank intermediation including private credit, and an AI boom driving unprecedented, increasingly debt-financed infrastructure investment. The FSB published 12 flexible Sound Practices for Responsible AI adoption covering governance, lifecycle management, cyber-risk mitigation, and third-party oversight; it is also examining cross-border stablecoin vulnerabilities building on 2023 recommendations and a 2025 review, with a G20 summary highlighting monitoring, implementation, and distress/failure planning. The FSB issued for consultation eight principles for modernising regulatory and supervisory frameworks—to be finalised in 2027 and explicitly not equivalent to deregulation—and its resolution peer review found only one in five FSB member jurisdictions fully compliant on temporary public sector funding for banks in resolution, with about half having material gaps.
