### 3. V-1 missile — Britannica
- **URL:** https://www.britannica.com/technology/V-1-missile
- **Summary:** German jet-propelled missile of WWII, forerunner of modern cruise missiles. Over 8,000 launched against London (13 June 1944 – 29 March 1945), ~2,400 hitting the target area.
