SCU’s current Procedure gives effect to the Governance Documents Rule and applies to all staff developing Rules, Policies, Procedures or Guidelines (RPPG). It sets minimum drafting standards, stakeholder consultation (waivable by an Executive or the Chair, Academic Board), Policy Advisor assessment for Rules/Policies excluding HR Policy, endorsement by the relevant Executive/Chair, Academic Board or Director, and approval by a Delegated Authority under the Delegations Rule; approved RPPGs must be published to the SCU Policy Library within 10 working days, and Work Units must not duplicate original RPPG files. It also covers implementation, compliance (staff via Heads of Work Units under the Code of Conduct; students via relevant Rules/Policies), annual review lists to Executives with triennial review at minimum and possible three-year reset, rescission, record keeping, and role responsibilities; the Vice-Chancellor may approve departures.
