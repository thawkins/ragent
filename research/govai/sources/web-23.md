Data Governance Automation embeds governance policies directly into data infrastructure, pipelines, and analytics platforms so automated controls can continuously classify data, validate quality, govern access, enforce policies, and monitor/alert on issues across ERP, analytics, and operational systems. Its applications in finance and enterprise data management include master data governance for GL and procurement, multi-entity and multi-currency data governance, and data governance integration, while supporting principles such as segregation of duties and benchmarks like the data governance maturity model. Benefits include improved data accuracy and reliability, consistent policy enforcement, transparency, faster detection of quality issues, scalability, and continuous improvement through programs such as data governance continuous improvement and an automation governance model.
