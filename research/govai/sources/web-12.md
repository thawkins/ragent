Southern Cross University’s Rule, made by Council under clause 52 of the Southern Cross University By-Law 2005 (NSW), establishes authorities and principles for developing and reviewing Rules, Policies, Procedures, and Guidelines and defines the Governance Document Hierarchy: Australian Laws, By-Laws, Rules, Policies, Procedures, then Guidelines, with higher documents prevailing. It applies to all University members, requires Rules/Policies to be reviewed at least every three years, makes compliance mandatory from the effective date until rescission or supersession, and provides that documents effective 13 April 2012 continue until amended or rescinded. The Director, Governance Services may approve minor amendments that do not change content or meaning, and New South Wales law applies.
