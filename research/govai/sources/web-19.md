Automated data governance uses AI-driven tools and workflows to automatically discover, classify, monitor, and enforce policies across an organization—covering data discovery/classification, metadata management, lineage/provenance, policy-as-code enforcement, stewardship, and integrated governance platforms—to support continuous compliance with regulations such as GDPR, CCPA, and HIPAA and improve data quality and access. The article cites 2024 Gartner Insights predicting that by 2027, 60% of organizations will fail to realize the anticipated value of their AI use cases due to incohesive data governance frameworks, and Forrester’s Data Strategy & Insights 2023 Survey finding fewer than 10% of enterprises are advanced in insights-driven capabilities largely due to ineffective governance. It also presents Bayview Financial Services, based in Florida, which during migration from on-premise infrastructure to a Snowflake cloud data warehouse used OvalEdge alongside Matillion to automate data quality rule execution, issue ticketing, real-time error detection/routing, and metadata/compliance workflows, achieving improved data accuracy and confidence, auto-monitoring and error resolution within minutes instead of hours, reduced manual data handling, and faster issue escalation.
