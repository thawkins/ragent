According to Gartner (2022), roughly 85% of enterprise AI projects never reach production, which the article attributes mainly to neglecting governance systems around models. It defines governed AI as observable, auditable, human-supervised, rollback-ready, and owned by the team, organized into five pillars: data governance, decision ownership, human-in-loop checkpoints, observability, and rollback architecture. KORIX estimates governance adds 20–30% engineering effort but is about 3x harder to retrofit later, and offers a 21-Day AI Pilot that builds governed production systems with audit trails, human checkpoints, and rollback capabilities.
