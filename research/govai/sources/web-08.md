```text
Automated data governance is framed as necessary because manual governance cannot keep up with enterprises running hundreds of systems, thousands of assets, and daily schema changes under regulations including GDPR, BCBS 239, HIPAA, and Brazil’s LGPD. The article argues automation pays off in metadata harvesting, schema change detection, ML-based sensitive-data classification, and policy-as-code, but only on a foundation of accurate structural metadata and enterprise data modeling; ER/Studio addresses this by reverse-engineering production databases such as Oracle, SQL Server, PostgreSQL, and Snowflake into models in hours rather than weeks, publishing metadata directly to catalogs like Microsoft Purview, Collibra, and Alation, and supporting forward engineering, impact analysis, ERbert natural-language model creation, and multi-user collaboration. It recommends a phased approach: establish accurate models, automate harvesting and catalog publishing, add classification and schema monitoring, then implement policy-as-code for high-risk domains.
```
