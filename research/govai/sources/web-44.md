Architecture governance has shifted from waterfall-era bureaucratic frameworks like TOGAF and Zachman—sidelined amid agile/DDD decentralization and causing fragmentation and technical debt (the author cites rearchitecting an app within 3 years)—to a strategic enabler; Gartner reports over 75% of enterprises are restarting or reinventing architecture programs. Modern practices—DevOps/CI-CD policy-as-code, microservices federated governance, cloud-native internal developer platforms/IaC, AI bill of materials and runtime behavioral policies, shift-left security, and data mesh—require automation-first compliance, domain ownership, platform engineering, strategic guardrails/ADRs, metrics such as deployment frequency, MTTR, and architecture fitness function compliance, plus psychological safety. The article advocates outcome-based “governed agility,” embedding governance in tools, processes, and culture to balance speed and stability and tie architecture to business value.
