AWS's DevOps Guidance defines automated governance as the strategic implementation of policies, processes, and tools to manage and control IT operations. It supports standardized, consistent risk management, compliance, and security; reduces manual intervention; improves scalability; and promotes best practices across managed environments. It balances agility and control, providing assurance and accountability while enabling innovation and rapid deployment.
