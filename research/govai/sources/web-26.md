```text
Workday argues that manual data governance cannot keep pace with modern data volumes and cites Gartner’s prediction that 80% of data governance strategies will fail by 2027 without a catalyst for change. It defines data governance automation as code-driven workflows for continuous policy enforcement, metadata management, and lineage tracking, organized around four pillars—data quality, data stewardship, data protection/compliance, and data management—with drivers including GDPR/CCPA regulatory pressure, data complexity, faster decisions, risk management, and scalability. AI capabilities such as NLP, ML, predictive risk scoring, automated classification, and intelligent recommendations enable proactive, continuous assurance, and the article recommends a unified platform with centralized metadata, a uniform policy engine, live lineage visualization, an elastic integration layer, and a collaborative stewardship workspace, illustrated by finance, healthcare, retail, HR, and higher-education use cases.
```
