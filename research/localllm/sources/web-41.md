Zen 5 (codenamed "Nirvana") is a 4-nanometer CPU microarchitecture developed by AMD, first revealed on the company's roadmap in May 2022. As the successor to Zen 4, it launched for mobile processors in July 2024 and for desktop processors in August 2024, and is currently fabricated on TSMC's N4P process.
