Nvidia's GeForce RTX 50 series is a line of consumer graphics cards succeeding the GeForce 40 series. Announced at CES 2025, the series launched in January 2025 with the RTX 5070, RTX 5080, and RTX 5090, and is built on Nvidia's Blackwell architecture.
