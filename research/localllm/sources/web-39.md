According to Bloomberg's Mark Gurman (via BGR), Apple is planning a major Mac roadmap for 2026 after CEO Tim Cook hinted no new Macs are coming in 2025. Early 2026 should bring MacBook Pro models with M5 Pro and M5 Max chips—promising big graphics and AI gains over the recently released M5 MacBook Pro—possibly alongside an M5 MacBook Air; mid-2026 could see an M5/M5 Pro Mac mini and M5 Max/M5 Ultra Mac Studio. By late 2026, Apple is rumored to launch a low-cost MacBook powered by an iPhone chip and an M6 MacBook Pro featuring an all-new design, OLED display, and touchscreen, though Gurman is uncertain whether the M6 Pro and M6 Max versions will arrive in 2026 or slip to early 2027.
