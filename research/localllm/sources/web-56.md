"Attention Is All You Need" is a landmark 2017 machine learning paper by eight Google researchers—Ashish Vaswani, Noam Shazeer, Niki Parmar, Jakob Uszkoreit, Llion Jones, Aidan Gomez, Łukasz Kaiser, and Illia Polosukhin (all listed as equal contributors in randomized order)—that introduced the transformer architecture, building on the attention mechanism proposed by Bahdanau et al. in 2014. Published June 12, 2017, the paper replaced recurrence with scaled dot-product self-attention, multi-head attention, and sinusoidal positional encoding, enabling full parallelization on GPUs; its original 100M-parameter model was trained on WMT 2014 English–German (4.5M sentences) and English–French (36M sentences) datasets using 8 NVIDIA P100 GPUs, surpassing 2017 state-of-the-art translation results at lower training cost. Although initially aimed at improving seq2seq machine translation, the transformer became the foundational architecture for modern large language models (BERT, GPT, ChatGPT) and spread to vision, speech, and multimodal systems (e.g., DALL-E, Stable Diffusion 3, Sora); as of 2026 the paper has been cited over 250,000 times, ranking among the top ten most-cited papers of the 21st century, and all eight authors subsequently left Google to join other companies or found startups. The title references the Beatles song "All You Need Is Love."
