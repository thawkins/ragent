RDNA 3 is a GPU microarchitecture designed by AMD, released alongside the Radeon RX 7000 series on December 13, 2022. In addition to powering the RX 7000 series graphics cards, the architecture is featured in AMD-designed SoCs used in handheld and compact gaming devices, including the Asus ROG Ally, Lenovo Legion Go, and the Steam Machine.
