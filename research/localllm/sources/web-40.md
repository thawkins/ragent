Bloomberg's Mark Gurman writes in his latest "Power On" newsletter that Apple's M6 chip—the company's first 2nm Apple Silicon—could arrive "potentially sooner than people anticipate," justifying the prediction by noting there was only a five-month gap between the M3 and M4 launches, and that the M5 was announced in October across three devices. While redesigned MacBook Pro models aren't expected until the end of 2026 (and M5 Pro/M5 Max launches are anticipated in the first half of 2026), the M6 could debut earlier in other products such as a Mac mini refresh or an iPad Pro—mirroring how the M4 powered Apple's flagship tablets before reaching Macs. The Wccftech article (by Omar Sohail) also notes it has been three years since Apple released two MacBook Pro lineups in a single year (2023), and cautions readers that Gurman has been wrong before, so the timeline should be treated skeptically.
