This HeroDevs guide advises enterprises to migrate from .NET 6 (released November 2021; end-of-life November 2024) to .NET 8 (released November 2023; LTS) for continued security and compliance, performance and scalability gains, cloud-native/container/observability improvements, and C# 12 support. It outlines migration steps including compatibility audits, SDK/CI updates, changing target frameworks from net6.0 to net8.0, upgrading NuGet dependencies, fixing breaking changes, testing/QA, deployment prep, and post-migration monitoring, while warning about breaking changes in middleware/APIs, trimming/publishing, container images, core libraries, and EF Core, as well as issues like third-party package compatibility and obsolete features such as BinaryFormatter. For teams unable to migrate immediately, HeroDevs offers Never-Ending Support for .NET 6 with critical patches, compatibility fixes, and extended security.
