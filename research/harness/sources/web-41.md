Roomote is a source-available, self-hosted AI coding teammate that can investigate code, answer repo questions, and open real PRs inside infrastructure you control. Everything stays visible to your team and ships through your normal review workflow.
It isn't a copilot or an IDE. It's a shared engineering teammate you can run, inspect, and adapt for your own team.Who is it for?
Roomote is built for engineering teams that want autonomous coding while keeping their source, prompts, and workflow visible and under their control.
It's also a great fit for individual developers who want a cloud agent they own and can run cheaply, especially on open-weight models.
Engineers use it for answers, scoped fixes, migrations, investigations, and reviewable PRs. Leaders use it to make AI engineering adoption easier to trust.Is this open source?
It's source-available under the Fair Core License, Version 1.0, ALv2 Future License. You can read it, self-host it, modify it, redistribute it, and use it for any permitted purpose, including internal use, education, research, and professional services.
The main limits are that you can't offer Roomote or substantially similar functionality as a competing commercial product or service, and you can't bypass license-key-protected functionality, namely going beyond 10 users in your deployment.
Every release also gets an irrevocable Apache 2.0 license on its second anniversary so anyone can use it into the future.We could just build this ourselves, no?
You could. Seems easy, right? Maybe even exciting. But as we learned, going beyond 'harness in the cloud' to something that reliably works end-to-end, is easy to use and doesn't keep breaking is quite hard. Oh, and building and testing integrations is super fun, right?
That effort adds up, and distracts you from your actual mission.What about first-party tools like Claude Tag?
Those are genuinely good, and if you don't mind being tied to a single vendor and subject to their pricing and availability whims, go for it.
Roomote is model-agnostic — bring Claude, bring GPT, bring an open-weight model — and it's self-hosted, so you own your destiny.I already use Cursor/Copilot/Claude Code/something else with a C. Why would I need Roomote?
Those are great editor tools for writing code in an IDE. Roomote is a cloud agent that runs in your own infrastructure, works across the systems you already use, and returns reviewable outputs anyone can read. The two are complementary.What harness do you use?
Currently we use OpenCode. It's reliable, powerful, fast and token-efficient (first-party harnesses seem very token hungry – who would have thunk?). We may change or add alternatives later, but it goes very far.How does it fit into our existing dev workflow?
Roomote is built to drop into the workflow you already have. It plugs into your chat app, source control host, issue tracker, docs, observability, data stack and simply starts working. It uses your skills, follows your agent guidance, uses your PR templates (and you can even customize its tone of voice to blend in with your people).What keeps it from breaking something? I've been burned by AI that ships junk.
Roomote works in isolated environments you control, uses the same repos and tools your team does, and pushes repository changes back through normal review paths instead of bypassing them.
Before it calls work done, it can run the actual app, self-review its changes, and attach proof like previews or screenshots when the task needs them.
You still keep control of what ships. The point is to make AI engineering inspectable, not lower your bar.Can multiple people use it at the same time?
Yes. Different teammates can run unlimited tasks in parallel, and Roomote also supports live multi-user tasks when multiple people need to work through the same issue together (including in chat threads).
