organizations gain fleetwide security and governance in a unified portal.

agreement entered with Microsoft, date of purchase, and the currency exchange rate. Prices are calculated based on US
dollars and converted using London closing spot rates that are captured in the two business days prior to the last
business day of the previous month end. If the two business days prior to the end of the month fall on a bank holiday in
major markets, the rate setting day is generally the day immediately preceding the two business days. This rate applies

## Frequently asked questions

[Frequently asked questions about Azure pricing][203]
* Is Foundry Free?
  The Foundry platform is free to use and explore. Individual features customers access and consume are billed at their
  normal rates.
* Do I need an Azure account to use Foundry?
  You do not need an Azure account to explore Foundry. However, before you can start building agents, you will need an
  Azure subscription.
* How is Foundry billed?
  Each service and product available in Foundry has its own billing model and price.
* What is the Microsoft Agent pre‑purchase plan and how does it work?
  
  The Microsoft Agent pre‑purchase plan allows you to save on Microsoft Foundry and Copilot Credit costs by purchasing
  Agent Commit Units (ACUs) up front in a 1‑year metered plan at discounted tiers and using them flexibly across the
  included Microsoft services.
