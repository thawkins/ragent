LLR Partners’ GrowthBit outlines five AI pricing models for software CEOs—token/consumption (used by OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google), SaaS plus consumption, AI premium tiers, outcome-based, and AI agent pricing—noting no single model has emerged as a clear winner. It weighs tradeoffs: consumption aligns revenue with inference costs but hurts predictability; hybrid models are common but add complexity; premium tiers are easy to implement but risk margin pressure from power users; outcome-based pricing requires measurable outcomes; and agent pricing shifts the conversation from IT spend to larger labor spend/TAM but is hard to measure. The article warns that giving AI away for free is the biggest mistake because every prompt, workflow, and agent action carries a cost, and advises building contract flexibility as token costs fall and planning for sales friction; it says a customer getting 10x more value could pay 10x more than under the old seat model.
