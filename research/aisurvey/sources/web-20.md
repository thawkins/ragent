```text
Mixpanel launched Agent Intelligence, an early-access product that links AI-agent activity to customer behavior and business outcomes by tracking agent conversations under the same user identity in funnels, replays, cohorts, and retention reports, while also recording cost, latency, error metrics, and turn-by-turn conversations including tool calls. Mixpanel CTO Anant Gupta said it aims to answer ROI questions such as whether agents move users through funnels or create friction, and Sprout Social’s Blake Kurinsky said it replaces tracking each agent interaction as a separate custom event with a full-conversation view. The launch accompanied broader Mixpanel updates including an expanded Mixpanel Agent, no-code experimentation, Context Engine updates, and AI-powered data governance; Mixpanel says it is used by more than 29,000 companies.
```
