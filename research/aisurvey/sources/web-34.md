```text
Mistral publicly released the open weights of its Large 4 model (internally ML4, nicknamed “Le Chonk”), a roughly one-trillion-parameter multimodal model with fewer parameters active per task, after an October 6 preview and three weeks of additional reinforcement learning and testing with government and corporate users; it targets cybersecurity teams that want to self-host sensitive workloads. Mistral says the release improves coding, reasoning, and cybersecurity capabilities, and Orange Cyberdefense has partnered with Mistral to integrate a cybersecurity-focused version into its products. Mistral acknowledges the open-weight trade-off: users gain control and customization, but safeguards can be removed and access cannot realistically be withdrawn.
```
