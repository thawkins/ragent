AI search monetization lets publishers earn through direct licensing, crawler-facing ads, paid brand coverage, subscriptions, syndication, or controlled marketplaces, ideally via a mix that preserves editorial approval; paid coverage becomes advertising if the buyer controls wording or outcomes. Referral traffic is weakening: Pew found Google click rates of 8% with AI summaries vs. 15% without, Ahrefs found 34.5% lower CTR for top results with AI Overviews, and Semrush reported AI Overviews on 13.14% of queries in March 2025, up from 6.49% in January. Digiday reported ChatGPT page views across 3,700+ publishers rising from 363,000 in August 2024 to 3 million in January 2025 and 5.3 million by April 2025, at 0.74% referrals per content scrape, while Similarweb showed YoY referral growth of 99.4% for The New York Times, 503.9% for People and USA Today, 173% for CNN, and 213.6% for Fox News—yet AI referrals remained under 1% of traffic for large news publishers.
