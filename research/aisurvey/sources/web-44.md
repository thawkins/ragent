```text
HITRUST argues that AI in regulated industries requires governance and security that protect confidentiality, integrity, and availability through clear ownership, risk tiering, access limits, human oversight, testing, and ongoing monitoring. It highlights stricter safeguards for agentic AI—least-privilege access, approval for high-impact actions, logging, and isolation—citing IBM’s Cost of a Data Breach Report 2026 finding that 92% of organizations experiencing an AI-related breach lacked proper AI access controls. The piece applies these principles to healthcare, financial services, and business services, and promotes HITRUST AI Risk Management and HITRUST AI Security Assessment and Certification.
```
