The Ada Lovelace Institute discussion paper *Beyond the Bubble* maps uncertainty around a possible AI market correction, noting Goldman Sachs’ estimate of $1.8 trillion in cumulative AI investment since 2022 and projected global AI investment above $1 trillion in 2026 alone. It warns that debt-fuelled capex, circular financing, market concentration, leverage and uncertain returns could trigger a correction, while policy preparation remains underdeveloped. Drawing on desk research and a June 2026 expert workshop with more than 20 policymakers and experts, it sets out four scenarios—business-as-usual/normalisation, sectoral crash, systemic crisis and dramatic take-off—to help governments and civil society prepare, and stresses that whether AI is financially overvalued is distinct from whether the technology will deliver long-term value.
