```text
AI model optimization aims to make models faster, smaller, and more accurate without sacrificing task performance through techniques such as hyperparameter tuning, data preprocessing, pruning, quantization, and fine-tuning, ideally across the AI development lifecycle. Reported effects include quantization shrinking models by 75%+ (typically 75–80% with under 2% accuracy loss), pruning removing 30–50% of parameters, distillation reaching 90–95% of teacher-model performance, and combined pruning-plus-quantization yielding models 4–5x smaller and 2–3x faster. Cited tools and frameworks include Optuna, Ray Tune, TensorRT, ONNX Runtime, XGBoost, Intel OpenVINO/oneDNN (3–8x gains), and Google Cloud AI Platform (up to 80% inference-time reduction), with cases such as a bank cutting inference time 73%, an e-commerce recommendation engine using 40% less compute, and a legal firm analyzing 100,000+ documents in under a minute.
```
