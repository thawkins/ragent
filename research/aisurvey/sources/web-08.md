In a LinkedIn post, Vikas Pai argues that AI is entering production faster than enterprises can observe, govern, and control it, shifting the key question from “Is my system healthy?” to “Is my AI behaving correctly, safely, efficiently, and within policy?” He describes Enterprise AI Observability as a critical emerging 5-layer stack: traditional observability; AI observability (prompt/response tracing, token usage, LLM performance, RAG quality, hallucinations, agent/tool calls, evaluations, model drift, cost); AI governance and runtime control (policy enforcement, model governance, risk, compliance, auditability, agent governance); AI runtime security (prompt injection, data leakage, malicious tool calls, agent abuse, MCP security, unauthorized model access); and AI production management (AI SRE, Agent Operations, AI FinOps, incident/change management, agent identity, AI service catalog, reliability). He frames the evolution as infrastructure observability → application observability → AI observability → AI production management, with winning platforms assessing whether AI is reliable, accurate, secure, compliant, cost-efficient, and explainable—and whether enterprises can operate AI at scale with the same confidence as critical business systems.
