In a Techstrong AI Leadership conversation, EY Distinguished Technologist Rick Ross tells host Mike Vizard that enterprise AI success depends on organizational readiness—people, processes, training, operating models, and the capacity to redesign workflows—not just buying tools. Ross says pilots often reveal that conventional ROI and NPV models fit poorly, so leaders should narrow initial focus to specific processes/capabilities and stage investments by risk appetite and resources. He also stresses governing software-agent lifecycles (onboarding, role changes, retirement), independent validation and architecture oversight, and protecting IP through architectural choices such as combining externally hosted models with localized processing for sensitive work; leaders should move deliberately with clear ownership, trained people, and safeguards rather than wait for all uncertainty to disappear.
