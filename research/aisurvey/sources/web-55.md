Deployflow’s guide argues that AI-agent ROI should come from solving one high-friction, measurable workflow with strong system fit, governance, human oversight, and a defined handover—not from choosing the most advanced tool or pursuing broad automation. It recommends measuring ROI through time saved, lower manual effort, faster cycle times, and capacity gains rather than headcount alone, citing OECD-reviewed studies showing 5%–25% productivity gains in support, software development, and consulting; its formula is (annual hours removed × loaded hourly cost) − build/run/oversight costs, illustrated by a service desk handling 400 requests/month at 12 minutes and £45/hour, where 60% no-escalation resolution removes about £25,900/year but against a £40,000 build and £1,200/month run does not pay back on labour alone in year one. It advises starting with one visible, high-volume workflow, defining success and payback thresholds before build, and using controlled pilots/sandboxes consistent with World Economic Forum guidance.
