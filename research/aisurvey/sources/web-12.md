```text
AI agent governance is needed as enterprises deploy hundreds of autonomous agents that can execute tasks such as bank transfers, document edits, and API calls, creating risks including data leaks, data poisoning, jailbreaks, credential theft, bias, and compliance violations. Effective governance requires centralized oversight, a complete agent inventory with unique identity, ownership, scope, autonomy level, and a designated human owner/business unit/risk officer; lifecycle monitoring; strict access controls; immutable audit trails; human-in-the-loop checkpoints for high-risk decisions; and clear metrics, roles, and stop authority. Vendors cited include Microsoft Copilot Studio, Salesforce Agentforce, and Lyzr, and the article notes governance must address privacy regulations such as GDPR and CCPA.
```
