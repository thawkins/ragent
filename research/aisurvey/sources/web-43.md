AI unit economics measures AI cost per unit of work and business value; Epoch AI reports model-capability prices falling 9x–900x per year while total AI spend rises because consumption grows faster than prices fall and reasoning models bill hidden thinking as output tokens. Yuriy Prykhodko of AWS Enterprise Support and the Cloud Intelligence Dashboards project presented a three-layer KPI model built on cost allocation, total AI cost, and governance: Layer 1 discount capture (cache hit rate, batch share, commitment coverage, model mix), Layer 2 cost per million tokens per application as a bridge metric, and Layer 3 business unit cost/value, culminating in a “one-more-dollar” scale test. The article cites Amazon Bedrock cache reads at a 90% discount and batch inference at 50% below on-demand, with SageMaker Savings Plans up to 64%, and describes Cloudaware’s FOCUS-based dataset spanning Bedrock, Vertex AI, Microsoft Foundry, OpenRouter, Anthropic, and OpenAI.
