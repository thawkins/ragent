Nomura’s Kieran White argues that despite massive AI spending—$582B in global corporate AI investment and $110B in trailing-12-month generative-AI revenue—only 13% of organizations report significant enterprise-level GenAI value and 40% of implemented projects miss goals. The hidden driver is “tokenomics”: tokens are roughly three-quarters of a word and behave like volatile input costs, with non-linear consumption, context inflation (a single enterprise query can use over 6,000 tokens before the user question, with over 85% of cost invisible), agentic multiplication, and a pricing cliff; every 10% token-price reduction yields 12–18% more tokens consumed, and enterprise AI budgets grew ~320% from 2024 to 2026. White recommends governing tokens like capital, rightsizing models (a compact model on dedicated hardware at ~$50/day for 100M tokens versus ~$1,560/day on a frontier API, a 32x difference), and considering on-premises inference, which can save over 50% versus APIs over three years.
