In a Techstrong AI Leadership conversation, EY Distinguished Technologist Rick Ross told host Mike Vizard that enterprise AI success depends less on buying tools than on organizational readiness—people, processes, training, operating-model change, and preparation for scale. Ross said many firms begin with productivity gains but then need to redesign workflows and business models; traditional ROI/NPV methods may not fit AI, so leaders should narrow initial focus to a specific process or capability and assess outcomes against desired business value, with investment priorities varying by resources and risk appetite (short-, medium-, and long-term bets vs. cautious waiting). He also stressed governing software-agent lifecycles (onboarding, role changes, retirement), independent validation and oversight by enterprise/solution architects, and architectural choices that protect IP by deciding which workloads use externally hosted models versus local processing.
