```text
J.P. Morgan Asset Management’s long-term capital market assumptions forecast that AI will raise U.S. total factor productivity growth by about 40 bps to 1.2% annually and capital stock growth by about 30 bps to 2.3%, lifting labor productivity by 50 bps to 2.2% and helping U.S. GDP return to 2%+ growth over 10–15 years. Although only 6% of U.S. work hours are currently AI-assisted versus over two-thirds of tasks theoretically exposed, it expects adoption to reach about 40% of today’s tasks by 2040, with the largest gains in the 2030s; the TFP uplift exceeds the CBO’s 10 bps estimate but sits in the lower half of the OECD’s 27–85 bps range. It cites nearly $1.6 trillion in cumulative U.S. hyperscaler capex since 2023 (over $2 trillion globally), projects U.S. AI capex rising from over $800 billion in 2026 to nearly $1.2 trillion by 2030, and argues investors should balance exposure across the AI value chain, funding cycle, public and private markets.
```
