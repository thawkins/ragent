Meta on Thursday launched **Muse Spark Open**, an open-weight AI model it claims rivals and sometimes outperforms leading Chinese open-weight models; Meta chief of AI **Alexandr Wang** called it “the most capable US open-weight model to date.” Meta says it is built for coding and agentic work, excels at long-horizon tasks, complex software engineering, and multi-step reasoning, includes “strong safeguards,” and competes with **Alibaba’s Qwen, Moonshot AI’s Kimi K3, and Z.ai’s GLM**. The model is optimized for **Apple’s M5 Ultra chips**—an unusual partnership—and can run on **two Mac Studios with M5 Ultra chips** to create a desktop “supercomputer,” though it requires significant computing power; the launch came days after Meta announced a new closed model it said rivalled top **ChatGPT** and **Claude** versions.
