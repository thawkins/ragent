Kore.ai’s blog argues that AI observability is essential as autonomous agents handle mission-critical enterprise workflows, citing Gartner’s prediction that 40% of enterprise workloads will be managed by autonomous AI agents by 2027. It defines AI observability as real-time capture and correlation of reasoning traces, model activations, tool/API calls, data access, latency, and output evaluations into execution graphs, organized around five pillars—Cognition, Traceability, Performance, Security, and Governance—to support auditability, drift/hallucination/bias detection, security, and compliance with GDPR, HIPAA, SOC2, and the EU AI Act. Kore.ai says its Enterprise AI Agent Platform embeds full-stack observability through agent-level tracing, performance and behavior analytics, configuration/policy monitoring, runtime security and access governance, and Responsible AI controls.
