A Forbes report cited in the post warns that AI data-center growth is colliding with debt obligations and rising financing costs, noting that nearly half of data-center bonds issued in 2026 carry below-investment-grade ratings. CoreWeave is highlighted: its shares reportedly fell 25% in the week ending October 16, about $4 billion of its corporate debt traded below 80 cents on the dollar, and bonds for data centers it leases saw yields rise to roughly 11% versus about 7% for BB+ debt—a four-percentage-point risk premium. The post says upfront GPU/infrastructure costs, rapid equipment obsolescence, and refinancing/utilization/counterparty risks make smaller AI cloud providers especially vulnerable, favoring projects with strong counterparties, durable contracts, and disciplined capital structures.
