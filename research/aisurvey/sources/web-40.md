The article argues that aggregate cloud spend or GPU counts obscure AI economic viability and recommends workload-normalized unit economics: cost per 1M output tokens for LLM serving, cost per effective FLOP/trained token incorporating Model Flops Utilization (MFU) for training, and cost per 1,000 queries for RAG. It cites benchmarks of $0.04–$0.08/1M tokens for Llama-3 8B, $0.45–$0.75/1M for Llama-3 70B, $0.80–$1.20/1B trained tokens for a 70B FSDP model, and $0.15–$0.35/1k RAG queries; raising MFU from 25% to 50% halves training unit cost, with >55% targeted. It describes extracting vLLM Prometheus metrics into Grafana/PromQL cost calculations and claims OneSource Cloud’s single-tenant bare-metal GPU clusters with flat-rate pricing, no egress fees, and RoCE v2 reduce unit costs.
