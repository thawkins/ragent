```text
The article argues that multimodal AI—systems handling text, images, audio, and video—requires evaluation beyond single-modality testing because cross-modal failures can occur, such as correctly identifying an image object but describing it incorrectly. It recommends testing image understanding and generation, text accuracy and instruction-following, audio robustness to accents/noise and speech output, video temporal consistency, and consistency across modalities. It also stresses continuous production monitoring for regressions after updates and a strategy combining automated tests, human review, representative datasets, clear metrics, and traceability to production components.
```
