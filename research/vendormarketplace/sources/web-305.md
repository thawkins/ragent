Anthropic expanded Claude with enterprise capabilities, including department-specific plugins (e.g., HR, investment banking), custom plugin development tools, and a marketplace where enterprises host and manage plugins; Anthropic’s Matt Piccolella described them as “mini apps” and said companies could build dozens, hundreds, or even thousands. Claude now connects to Google Drive, Gmail, DocuSign, and LegalZoom to operate within existing workflows, similar to its PowerPoint integration. Since Anthropic’s Jan. 30 Claude Cowork announcement, enterprise software stocks fell—ServiceNow -23%, Salesforce -22%, Snowflake -20%, Intuit -33%, Thomson Reuters -31%—while its Feb. 20 Claude Code Security launch preceded declines in CrowdStrike -7.2%, Zscaler -7.1%, and Palo Alto Networks -2.6%. OpenAI’s Frontier platform also lets users build AI agents that interact with company software, and Anthropic disclosed no pricing or rollout timeline beyond the announcement.
