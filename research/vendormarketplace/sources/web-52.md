```text
Anthropic launched Claude Marketplace on September 23, 2026, a public catalog with more than 2,000 connectors and plugins, organized into connectors/plugins, agents/products, and service partners. Launch partners include Atlassian, Google, Microsoft, Notion, and Salesforce for integrations; CrowdStrike, Cursor, Harvey, Hebbia, Legora, Lovable, and Snowflake for Claude-powered products purchasable with committed Anthropic spend; and Accenture, Boston Consulting Group, and Deloitte for consulting/systems integration. Developers can submit MCP/Agent Skills connectors or plugins via Anthropic documentation, product companies can apply for listing, and consulting/integration firms can join the Claude Partner Network. Anthropic has not disclosed review/approval details or whether Pro/Max subscribers get the same purchasing options as enterprise customers; the launch follows its recent free Claude Code cloud credits for subscribers.
```
