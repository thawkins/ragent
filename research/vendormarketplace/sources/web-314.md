On Wednesday, Anthropic reported elevated error rates across its Claude chatbot, API, and Claude Code, saying it began investigating at 10:53 a.m. ET, that login success rates stabilized around 12:30 p.m. ET, and that all systems were operational by 1:50 p.m. ET. Anthropic, founded in 2021 by former OpenAI researchers and executives and valued at $380 billion as of February, competes for enterprise AI market share with Google and OpenAI, which was valued at $850 billion in a late-March fundraising round.
