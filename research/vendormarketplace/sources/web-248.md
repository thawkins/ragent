This commit refactors connector-directory caching in just-every/code by replacing `AllConnectorsCacheKey` with `ConnectorDirectoryCacheContext` and `ConnectorDirectoryCacheKey`, adding `codex_home` to the context and updating call sites to use `cached_directory_connectors`. Dependencies `serde_json`, `tracing`, and dev-dependency `tempfile` are added.
