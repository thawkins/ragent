Anthropic ships an in-app directory of Claude skills, connectors, and plugins inside Claude Desktop; the tutorial advises browsing it before building by clicking the + next to Skills and selecting Browse skills, then searching real keywords like “presentation,” “alt text,” “expense,” or “email reply” and installing in one click so the skill is active in every new chat. Top recommended installs include frontend-design, skill-creator, docx/pptx/xlsx generators, and setup-cowork. Anthropic-published skills are reviewed and maintained, while third-party skills require checking the description, author, scripts/ folder, and connector access (e.g., Gmail, Drive, CRM); finding and installing one takes about 60–90 seconds and can prevent rebuilding already-shipped skills.
