OpenAI launched Codex plugins, a marketplace of over 20 integrations connecting its AI coding agent to tools including Figma, Notion, Gmail, Slack, and Google Drive, turning Codex into a workflow automation platform spanning design, communication, documentation, and coordination. Plugins are installable bundles that can include skills, app integrations, and MCP server configurations, and they work across the desktop app, CLI, and IDE extensions such as VS Code. OpenAI positions Codex as a competitor to Zapier and Make and plans to open third-party plugin development with self-serve publishing; plugins are available to Codex users with pricing varying by OpenAI plan and plugin requirements, though public self-publishing is not yet available.
