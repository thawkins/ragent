This commit is a renaming/refactoring change in OpenAI's Codex repository, consolidating plugin marketplace types: several `...Summary` types (e.g., `MarketplacePluginSourceSummary`, `PluginManifestInterfaceSummary`, `MarketplaceInterfaceSummary`, `ConfiguredMarketplaceSummary`, `ConfiguredMarketplacePluginSummary`, `PluginDetailSummary`) are renamed to drop the "Summary" suffix (e.g., `MarketplacePluginSource`, `PluginManifestInterface`, `ConfiguredMarketplace`, `PluginDetail`), and `load_marketplace_summary` becomes `load_marketplace`. The separate `install_policy` and `auth_policy` fields (types `MarketplacePluginInstallPolicy`/`MarketplacePluginAuthPolicy`) are replaced by a single `MarketplacePluginPolicy` struct with `installation` and `authentication` members, changing the marketplace.json serialization from top-level `"installPolicy"`/`"authPolicy"` keys to a nested `"policy": {"installation": ..., "authentication": ...}` object. Helper functions `plugin_manifest_name` and `plugin_manifest_paths` are removed in favor of manifest fields `manifest.name` and `manifest.paths`, and test fixtures for `plugin_list` and `plugin_read` are updated to the new JSON shape.
