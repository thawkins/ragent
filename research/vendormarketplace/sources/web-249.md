Role-based Codex plugins package domain-specific skills, connector bindings, and starter assets for sales, data analytics, product design, and financial markets; built with OpenAI subject matter experts, they are meant to be customized, and connector-backed plugins may contain placeholder app/connector IDs (e.g., REPLACE_WITH_SALESFORCE_APP_OR_CONNECTOR_ID, REPLACE_WITH_SITES_APP_OR_CONNECTOR_ID) that must be replaced with workspace-available IDs, while canonical shared platform connector IDs and templated_apps_* registry IDs should remain unchanged. The repo contains .agents/plugins/marketplace.json and plugins/sales, data-analytics, product-design, and financial-markets, each with .codex-plugin/plugin.json, optional .app.json and .mcp.json, skills/, assets/, and README.md. Sales connectors include Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Google Drive, Gmail, Outlook, Outreach, Clay, and ZoomInfo; data analytics includes Databricks, Snowflake, BigQuery, Hex, Amplitude, Mixpanel, Statsig, Metabase, ThoughtSpot, Google Drive, Slack, and Microsoft 365; product design uses Sites; financial markets includes FactSet, LSEG, Morningstar, Daloopa, Quartr, S&P, PitchBook, Slack, Google Drive, Gmail, SharePoint, and Teams. The MIT-licensed repository accepts contributions via fork and pull request, and OpenAI plans to expand it with more roles, workflows, and examples.
