Practical limits: image turns burn ChatGPT quota roughly 3–5× faster than text, images take 1–4 minutes each, custom sizes require longest edge ≤3840px, edges as multiples of 16, ratio ≤3:1, and 655,360–8,294,400 total pixels; support is macOS/Linux only, and the project is MIT-licensed.
