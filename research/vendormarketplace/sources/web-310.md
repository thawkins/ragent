Anthropic cut off OpenAI’s API access to Anthropic models on Tuesday after finding OpenAI violated its terms by accessing Anthropic’s tools in prohibited ways; Anthropic spokesperson Christopher Nulty said OpenAI technical staff used Claude Code ahead of GPT-5’s launch, violating commercial terms that bar developing another product/service, building AI models on Anthropic’s platform, or dissecting/replicating its technology. OpenAI allegedly used custom APIs to integrate Claude into its toolchain to assess programming/writing and safety-related queries (CSAM, self-harm, defamatory content), compare Claude with its in-house models, and fine-tune its systems; OpenAI CCO Hannah Wong called evaluating other AI systems industry standard and said it was disappointing given OpenAI’s API remains available to Anthropic, while Anthropic has not clarified how the restriction affects OpenAI’s evaluations. Separately, Anthropic plans weekly Claude Code caps starting Aug. 28 for the $20 Pro and $100/$200 Max tiers—existing five-hour quotas unchanged, with a general weekly cap and a model-specific allowance for Claude Opus 4, and Max users able to buy extra capacity at standard API pricing—after seven major or partial outages last month.
