Anthropic reported elevated error rates on Wednesday across its Claude chatbot, API, and Claude Code, beginning an investigation at 10:53 a.m. ET and saying around 12:30 p.m. ET that chatbot login success rates had stabilized as it worked to “fully resolve this issue”; by 1:50 p.m. ET, all systems were operational.
