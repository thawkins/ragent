Alongside it, OpenAI launched an enterprise plugin system with **20+ plugins** including Figma, Notion, Gmail, Google Drive, and Slack, plus **Codex Security**, which found nearly **800 critical vulnerabilities** across projects including Chromium and OpenSSL during testing on **1.2 million commits**.
