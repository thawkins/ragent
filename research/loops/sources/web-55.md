```text
This Substack post argues that enterprises—despite spending over $100B annually on "Customer 360" initiatives (MDM, CDPs, Data Clouds)—have built cost centers rather than profit engines, and proposes "agentic loops": AI systems layered on existing data infrastructure that observe outcomes, update policies, and act automatically. The author traces an evolution from MDM (trustworthy but batch/static) through Composable CDPs with Reverse ETL (activated but linear) to agentic loops structured around an OODA framework (Observe, Orient, Decide, Act, plus Learn), using the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to let AI agents securely access warehouses, clean rooms, and partner signals while enforcing consent and brand-safety guardrails. Key components include identity matching (UID2, Meta CAPI, Google Enhanced Conversions, LinkedIn CAPI), server-to-server conversions, bid/pacing/budget agents, and reinforcement-driven policy updates that cut decision latency from weeks to minutes. Reported results: retail saw −11% wasted impressions and +13% ROAS in 7 weeks; financial services improved match rates from 47% to 71% with −18% CAC in 90 days; B2B SaaS gained +23% pipeline at the same spend in 120 days. The post closes with a 90-day implementation plan (foundations in weeks 0–2, deterministic wins weeks 3–6, agents live weeks 7–10, compounding weeks 11–13), prioritizing decision latency, marginal ROAS, and match rate as core metrics, with single-digit lift expected by day 90 and steeper compounding by day 180.
```
