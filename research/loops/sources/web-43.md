This SAP learning lesson explains the "agentic loop," the repeating cycle that distinguishes AI agents from traditional linear software: agents operate in six stages—Perceive, Plan, Act, Observe, Reflect, and Repeat—taking a step, evaluating the result, and continuing until a task is complete or requires human escalation. Human touchpoints are built in by governance design, with organizations defining pause conditions such as decisions exceeding financial thresholds, customer-facing actions requiring review, exceptions outside policy boundaries, or regulatory approval requirements. An illustrative example shows an agent resolving an invoice with a 2.3% price discrepancy (within a 3% auto-resolution tolerance) by cross-referencing the purchase order and contract pricing in the ERP, while a 4.7% discrepancy would trigger escalation to a finance reviewer with full context. The lesson also describes multi-agent orchestration, in which a central orchestrating agent coordinates specialized agents for large cross-functional processes, and states that SAP's Joule serves as this orchestrating layer today, coordinating agents across Finance, HR, Supply Chain, Procurement, and Customer Experience, each grounded in the relevant SAP process context and data.
