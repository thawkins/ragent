AHE (Agentic Harness Engineering) is an open observability system for automatically evolving the harness around a fixed coding-agent base model, with changes confined to seven git-tracked NexAU components—systemprompt.md, code_agent.yaml, tool_descriptions/, tools/, middleware/, skills/, sub_agents/, plus LongTermMEMORY.md. Its three observability layers are Component, Experience (Agent Debugger distilling ~10M-token raw traces into layered sourced reports), and Decision (Evolve Agent proposing evidence-backed, falsifiable edits); across ten evaluate→analyze→improve iterations, AHE lifts Terminal-Bench 2 pass@1 from 69.7% to 77.0% on GPT-5.4, surpassing hand-written Codex (71.9%) and self-evolving ACE and TF-GRPO baselines. The frozen evolved harness transfers without re-evolution to SWE-bench-verified and four alternate base models, and the release is MIT-licensed, though Agent Debugger is only partially open-sourced due to company strategy.
