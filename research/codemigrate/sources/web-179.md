```text
A 450-person engineering org migrated 10M lines of proprietary code across 127 repositories from GitHub Copilot X to Codeium 2.0 over 14 weeks, coordinating 12 product teams and Java, Python, TypeScript, Go, and Rust codebases. It left Copilot X due to $1.2M annual cost for 450 seats, lack of on-prem compliance for healthcare/fintech clients, and a 4k token context that produced irrelevant suggestions for 38% of requests in repos over 500k lines; Codeium offered 16k context, on-prem deployment, 40% lower per-seat cost, and custom fine-tuning. The phased migration took 1.2k engineering hours, required manual rewrites for 12% of 12k custom snippets, faced 22% initial developer resistance (later 89% approval), and cut unplanned downtime by 70%. Results included suggestion acceptance rising from 62% to 78%, 42% lower annual AI coding tool spend after $180k in migration engineering hours, SOC 2/HIPAA compliance, 3 new enterprise client contracts in Q3, 92% developer satisfaction (up from 78%), 15% faster PR cycle times, 12% better boilerplate reduction over Copilot X (37% total vs pre-AI tools), zero AI-tooling compliance incidents versus 2 near-misses, and 35% lower suggestion edit time for junior developers after 40 hours of fine-tuning.
```
