GPT-Migrate is a full-scale architectural migration tool that ingests entire codebases, maps dependency graphs, translates code idiomatically, resolves equivalent libraries (e.g., suggesting Tokio or Serde for Node.js-to-Rust migrations), propagates context-aware refactors, and generates parallel test suites in the target language. It excels at structural complexity and boilerplate reduction but can hallucinate complex logic in edge cases—handling about 90% well, with the remaining 10% (e.g., complex async patterns or niche hardware integrations) potentially subtly wrong—and struggles with proprietary or obfuscated legacy code; large 100,000+ LOC migrations can take hours or days. Pricing is tiered by LOC: Starter $49/month up to 10,000 LOC, Professional $199/month up to 100,000 LOC, and Enterprise custom pricing for 1M+ LOC with on-premise deployment. Best for legacy monoliths moving to modern frameworks or dying-language migrations (e.g., Ruby to Go), not small scripts, air-gapped sensitive systems, or teams unwilling to manually review; alternatives include CodeConvert, RefactorAI, and LegacyLift, and its final verdict is 8.5/10 as a high-quality draft requiring human architect review.
