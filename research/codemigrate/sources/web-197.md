```text
TDAD (Test-Driven Agentic Development) is an open-source tool and benchmark methodology that builds an AST-based code–test dependency graph with weighted impact analysis to surface tests likely affected by an AI coding agent’s proposed change. On SWE-bench Verified with Qwen3-Coder 30B (100 instances) and Qwen3.5-35B-A3B (25 instances), its GraphRAG workflow reduced test-level regressions by 70% (6.08%→1.82%; 562→155 P2P failures) and, as an agent skill, improved resolution from 24% to 32%; TDD prompting alone instead increased regressions to 9.94%. An autonomous auto-improvement loop raised resolution from 12% to 60% on a 10-instance subset with 0% regression, and all code, data, and logs are public under MIT.
```
