Meta launched Muse Code in beta on Aug. 5, 2026, a terminal-based coding agent for macOS and Linux (no Windows, GUI, or IDE plugin) powered by Meta Superintelligence Labs’ Muse Spark 1.2, a 1M-token-context coding model co-trained with the agent harness. On Meta’s own benchmarks it placed second to Anthropic’s Claude Opus 5 on every chart shown—Terminal-Bench 2.1: 82.9% vs. 86.7%; DeepSWE: 59.3% vs. 65.0%; Meta Internal Coding Bench: 70.6% vs. 79.4%—and scored 54 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, tied with Grok 4.5 and behind Claude Opus 5 (61) and GPT-5.6 Sol (59). Pricing is $1.25/$4.25 per million input/output tokens on the standard tier, or a contributor tier at $0.10/$0.20 if developers let Meta train on their prompts and code; Meta says 7,000 internal engineers already use it weekly, and it offers crash-resuming local event logs and parallel sub-agents.
