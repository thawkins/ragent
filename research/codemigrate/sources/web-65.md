AHE (Agentic Harness Engineering) is an open observability system that automatically evolves the harness around a fixed coding-agent base model, optimizing system prompts, tool descriptions and implementations, middleware, skills, sub-agents, and long-term memory. It relies on three observability layers: component observability via NexAU’s seven orthogonal, git-tracked file-level components; experience observability via Agent Debugger distilling ~10M-token raw traces into layered sourced reports; and decision observability via Evolve Agent’s evidence-backed, impact-predicting edits falsified by next-iteration flipped tasks. Over ten evaluate→analyze→improve iterations, AHE raises Terminal-Bench 2 pass@1 from 69.7% to 77.0% on GPT-5.4, surpassing hand-written Codex (71.9%) and the self-evolving ACE and TF-GRPO baselines; its frozen harness transfers without re-evolution to SWE-bench-verified and four alternate base models, suggesting the evolved components encode general engineering experience rather than benchmark-specific tuning.
