The article argues that agentic AI can usefully augment—but not replace—architectural judgment in mainframe modernization, since such decisions are irreversible and carry long-term risk, regulatory, and financial consequences. It disputes Anthropic’s framing of IBM Z as transitional and COBOL/mainframe as things to escape, saying IBM Z is cutting-edge, stability-critical, and underpins the global economy, while rewriting COBOL into Java (1995) or Python (1991) is not always advisable and HLASM may remain necessary for speed and efficiency. It recommends using agentic AI as a coding/error-solving thought partner and architectural co-designer, with human engineers deciding the right platform mix—including what should not be modernized.
