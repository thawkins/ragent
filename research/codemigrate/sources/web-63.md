```text
AHE (Agentic Harness Engineering) is an open observability system that automatically evolves the harness around a coding agent while keeping the base model fixed, targeting system prompts, tool descriptions, tool implementations, middleware, skills, sub-agents, and long-term memory. It relies on three observability layers: NexAU’s git-tracked seven orthogonal file-level components; Agent Debugger’s layered, sourced reports distilled from ~10M-token raw traces (with drill-down to raw traces); and Evolve Agent’s evidence-backed, impact-predicting edits that are automatically falsified by next-iteration task flips. Across ten evaluate→analyze→improve iterations, AHE lifts Terminal-Bench 2 pass@1 from 69.7% to 77.0%, surpasses hand-written Codex (71.9%) and self-evolving ACE/TF-GRPO baselines, and produces a frozen harness that transfers without re-evolution to SWE-bench-verified and four alternate base models, indicating general engineering experience rather than benchmark-specific tuning. It runs rollouts in E2B sandboxes via SaaS (with concurrency caps) or self-hosted clusters, and its Agent Debugger is only partially open-sourced due to company strategy.
```
