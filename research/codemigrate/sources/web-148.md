Gemini CLI is an autonomous ReAct-loop agent with multi-step planning, file/shell/web-fetch actions, and deep Gemini/Google Search grounding, while Aider is a model-agnostic pair-programming tool that makes one logical change at a time, commits it, and waits for developer feedback, supporting 100+ LLM providers via LiteLLM (including Gemini) and local models via Ollama. Gemini CLI’s free tier ran from June 2025 to June 18, 2026—60 requests/min and 1,000/day via a personal Google account with Gemini 2.5 Pro and a 1M-token context—but from March 2026 free users were restricted to Flash models, and on June 18, 2026 Google discontinued free, Google AI Pro, and Google AI Ultra access, leaving full access only to paid Gemini Code Assist Standard ($19/user/month annual) or Enterprise ($45/user/month annual) customers; Apache 2.0 source remains public, with individuals directed to Antigravity CLI (Google’s closed-source Go-based replacement) or self-hosting. Aider itself is free and open source under Apache 2.0 with no subscription, tiers, usage limits, or platform fees, so costs come only from the chosen LLM provider (e.g., Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek) per token, or zero with Ollama.
