Enterprise modernization has shifted from a “should” to a “must” because legacy COBOL mainframes cannot directly support AI agents, U.S. technical debt is estimated at $1.5 trillion, and one customer cited a project that once would have taken six or seven years now needing completion in two. Agentic AI is both the driver and enabler: specialized, collaborating agents with human-in-the-loop checkpoints, validation, and traceability can compress migration and modernization into one step and cut project times, technical debt, and costs by up to 80%; using AWS Transform, Thomson Reuters reduced tech debt by 50% and costs by 30%, while Air Canada upgraded Node.js runtimes in days with 90% efficacy and roughly 80% lower project time and cost. The platform approach also emphasizes privacy and compliance through encryption, no training on shared data, optional in-environment transformations, and code/tests never leaving internal systems.
