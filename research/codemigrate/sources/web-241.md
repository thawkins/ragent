Google’s “Automating modernization with Antigravity” codelab, a companion to “How to automate modernization with Antigravity and multi-agent orchestration,” teaches orchestrating autonomous AI subagents in Google Antigravity to rebuild a legacy Express/Mongoose CRUD monolith (the “madhums demo”) into a TypeScript/ESM Next.js App Router app with MongoDB + strict Zod validation, NextAuth, and ShadCN UI/Tailwind. It covers an extensible Agent Skills Pack using progressive disclosure and YAML routing metadata; Router, Plan-and-Execute, and Reflexion patterns; strict input/skill/output contracts; and a five-phase pipeline: audit/reverse-engineering into docs/API_Contracts.md, docs/Business_Logic_Rules.md, docs/Data_Models.md, and docs/UI_Inventory.md; TDD Vitest harness and backend scaffolding; frontend scaffolding; and Browser Subagent-driven side-by-side parity plus adversarial verification with WebP recordings. Requirements include Antigravity IDE, Node.js v18+, Chrome, and the devrel-demos modernizing-expressjs repo; post-migration steps suggest Strangler Fig routing, _redirects.yaml SEO preservation, live data streaming, and OpenTelemetry observability.
