```text
AHE (Agentic Harness Engineering) is an open observability system for automatically evolving the harness around a fixed coding-agent base model, modifying components including system prompts, tool descriptions, tool implementations, middleware, skills, sub-agents, and long-term memory. It uses three observability layers—NexAU component observability over seven git-tracked file-level components, Agent Debugger experience observability that distills ~10M-token raw traces into layered sourced reports, and Evolve Agent decision observability that proposes evidence-backed edits predicted then automatically falsified by the next iteration’s flipped tasks. Across ten evaluate→analyze→improve iterations, AHE lifts Terminal-Bench 2 pass@1 from 69.7% to 77.0%, surpasses hand-written Codex (71.9%) and the self-evolving ACE and TF-GRPO baselines, and produces a frozen harness that transfers without re-evolution to SWE-bench-verified and four alternate base models, indicating the evolved components encode general engineering experience rather than benchmark-specific tuning; the release’s Agent Debugger is only partially open-sourced due to company strategy.
```
