Keegan-Michael Key (born March 22, 1971, in Southfield, Michigan) is an American actor, comedian, producer, and writer who co-created and co-starred with Jordan Peele in *Key & Peele* (2012–2015), winning one Primetime Emmy Award from ten nominations. His credits include *Mad TV* (2004–2009), *Playing House* (2014–2017), *Friends from College* (2017–2019), *Reboot* (2022), *Fargo* (2014), *Parks and Recreation* (2013–2015), *Schmigadoon!* (2021–2023), films such as *Horrible Bosses 2* (2014), *Pitch Perfect 2* (2015), *Dolemite Is My Name* (2019), *The Prom* (2020), and *Wonka* (2023), and voice roles in *The Lego Movie*, *Hotel Transylvania*, *Toy Story 4*, *The Lion King*, *The Super Mario Bros. Movie*, *Transformers One*, and *The Super Mario Galaxy Movie* (2026). He appeared as Luther at the 2015 White House Correspondents’ Dinner, starred in *Keanu* (2016), made his Broadway debut in *Meteor Shower* (2017), hosted *The Planet’s Funniest Animals* (2005–2008) and *Game On!* (2020), married Cynthia Blaise (1998–2017) and later Elisa “Elle” Pugliese (2018), and earned a BFA from the University of Detroit Mercy in 1993 and an MFA from Pennsylvania State University in 1996.
