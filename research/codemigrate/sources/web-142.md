An arXiv-published study by Shane Barrett (updated August 26, 2026) introduces SWE Refactor Bench, a 20-task suite pairing pre-migration repositories with target post-migration states to measure coding agents on whole-repository stack migrations spanning thousands of files and coordinated changes across languages, frameworks, and build systems. Given only a high-level migration objective and the starting codebase, frontier agents completed just 5.4% of runs end-to-end—below 6%—with high variance; the study attributes the gap to sustained multi-file coordination, where planning errors and stale downstream references compound. It recommends tracking expansion beyond 20 tasks and broader language coverage, per-stage planning/execution/validation success alongside aggregate pass rates, and reference implementations or replay tooling for reproducibility, noting parallels to long-horizon adaptation research such as *Adapting to Europe’s Climate Crisis*.
