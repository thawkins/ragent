```text
Nalar is a ground-up agent-serving framework from UT-Austin and Cisco Research (Marco Laju, Donghyun Son, Saurabh Agarwal, Nitin Kedia, Myungjin Lee, Jayanth Srinivasa, Aditya Akella) that separates workflow specification from execution while preserving full Python expressiveness. It uses auto-generated stubs to turn agent/tool invocations into futures carrying dependency and context metadata, a managed state layer that decouples logical state from physical placement for safe reuse/migration/retry, and a two-level control architecture with global policy computation plus local event-driven enforcement. Implemented in ~13,300 lines of Python, Nalar reports across three agentic workloads: 34–74% tail-latency cuts, up to 2.9× speedups, sustained 80 RPS where baselines fail, sub-50s average latency at 80 RPS, scaling to 130K futures with sub-500 ms control overhead, and policies implementable in as little as 12 lines of code.
```
