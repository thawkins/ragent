EverMind AI’s Raven is a pre-alpha “harness of harnesses” for recursive self-improvement: a Host Agent orchestrating built-in and third-party agents (via ACP, CLI, or OpenAI-compatible APIs, with presets for 13 third-party agents) and using EverOS for cross-session memory/context. Its built-in agents—Raven-Research, Raven-Code, Raven-Design, and Raven-Oncall—cover research, coding, visual design, and unattended workflow automation, with claimed benchmark leadership including Raven-Code topping DataAgentBench for data analysis (2026-08-24 Live), Raven-Design topping PresentBench, and Raven-Oncall outperforming Claude Code on quality/cost for AI4AI and success rate/cost for AI4S. Showcases include THRESHOLD, a playable Godot 4 FPS built autonomously in about 4 days over 42 rounds, and Raven RSI, which ran 172 nanochat pretraining runs across 7 rounds without crashes, reduced val_bpb by 5.8% within the same 20-minute single-GPU budget, cut dam-break overshoot by three orders of magnitude, and completed an FEA limit-load search in 8 bisection rounds. Architecture uses four decoupled strategy modules (Memory, Planning, Capability, Action) plus an experimental Curator for runtime self-evolution; SkillForge offers 114,190 skills, and the repo is Apache-2.0 with citation arXiv:2609.33439.
