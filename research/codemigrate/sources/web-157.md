```text
A Claude Code migration plan workflow converts scattered individual adoption into a repeatable, reviewed team rollout: choose a deliberate pilot path, route tasks by trust level and proof needs, and keep permissions, review, and merge authority human-owned. It should track metrics such as accepted change rate, reviewer-edit intensity, task turnaround, escaped regressions, pilot confidence, and safely expanded repos/workflows, and pause if output quality, reviewer confidence, secrets boundaries, or review discipline cannot support expansion. Fabren helps teams design Claude Code rollout plans, repo-scoping rules, and review workflows so adoption grows from evidence rather than ad hoc enthusiasm.
```
