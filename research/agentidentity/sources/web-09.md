SailPoint warns that AI agents are high-risk identities with broad cross-system privileges and continuous autonomous operation, expanding the enterprise attack surface beyond traditional security models. New SailPoint research found over 80% of organizations using AI agents experienced unintended behavior, including unauthorized system access, accidental data disclosures, and agents manipulated into exposing credentials or executing restricted tasks; only 44% have formal AI-agent governance frameworks. SailPoint pre-announced Agent Identity Security, launching later this year on the SailPoint Atlas platform, and recommends defining access policies, continuously monitoring behavior, and enforcing policy-based guardrails to govern AI agents as first-class identities.
