```text
The article argues that Agentic AI is a governance transformation, not just a technology risk, because autonomous agents access data, invoke tools, delegate authority, and act without direct human oversight. It says traditional model-centric AI governance falls short and that compliance requires identity-centric controls: AI agent identity, authentication with short-lived/scoped credentials, scoped/time-bound/revocable delegation, tool-level least-privilege authorization, and observable audit trails. It positions LoginRadius as a CIAM platform offering non-human identity lifecycle governance, strong authentication, fine-grained authorization, delegation tracking, centralized audit logging, and API-first extensibility for regulated Agentic AI deployments.
```
