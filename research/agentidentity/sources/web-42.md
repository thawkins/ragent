```text
AI agent identity is an autonomous system’s unique, verifiable digital identity, tied to specific privileges, contextual awareness, and continuous lifecycle governance; securing it requires identity frameworks for machine-speed autonomous reasoning, including delegation chains, contextual authorization, behavioral validation, unique cryptographic credentials, dynamic least-privilege access, behavioral analytics, secure delegation, API-first/Zero Trust integration, and governance. Organizations now commonly manage at least 45 machine identities per human user, and AI agents amplify non-human identity risks such as spoofing, stolen or long-lived credentials, malicious-input attacks, cascading tool/API dependencies, and self-permission changes, while the EU AI Act’s human-oversight requirements for high-risk systems may conflict with autonomous operation. Recommended controls include lifecycle management, OAuth 2.0, managed identities, encryption, human-in-the-loop checkpoints, red teaming, and cross-functional governance; Okta states its platform provides AI agent discovery, lifecycle management, and governance, with unique identities, immediate revocation, and audit logs of actions and reasoning chains.
```
