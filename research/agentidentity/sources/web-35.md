The CSA research note reports that NIST’s Center for AI Standards and Innovation launched the AI Agent Standards Initiative on Feb. 17, 2026, with three pillars: industry-led standards, open-source protocol interoperability, and research on AI agent security and identity. It follows a Jan. 8, 2026 RFI (NIST-2025-0035, comments closed Mar. 9, 2026) and connects to NCCoE’s Feb. 5, 2026 concept paper on AI agent identity/authorization, the mid-2025 COSAiS SP 800-53 overlays for single- and multi-agent systems, and NIST IR 8596 (Dec. 16, 2025). Citing an 81% attack success rate in NIST red-teaming, plus NIST AI 100-2 (March 2025), MITRE ATLAS v5.4.0 (Feb. 2026), and OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications 2026, the note urges enterprises not to wait for final guidance: audit agent identity, task-scoped permissions, and non-human audit logging; begin COSAiS gap analysis in AC, IA, AU, and SR control families; adopt OWASP’s “Least Agency”; and align with CSA MAESTRO, AI Organizational Responsibilities, CCM/AICM, and STAR.
