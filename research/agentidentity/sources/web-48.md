The Association for the Advancement of Artificial Intelligence (AAAI) is an international scientific society promoting AI research, responsible use, public understanding, AI education/training, and guidance for research planners and funders. Founded in 1979 as the American Association for Artificial Intelligence and renamed in 2007, it claims over 4,000 members worldwide and was early led by Allen Newell, Edward Feigenbaum, Marvin Minsky, and John McCarthy; leadership listings include President Stephen Smith, President-elect Michael Wooldridge, and Past President Francesca Rossi, with text noting Rossi served from July 2022 until Smith began in July 2024. AAAI sponsors conferences and symposia, supports 14 AI journals, publishes AI Magazine quarterly since 1980, and organizes the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence—considered one of the field’s top conferences—plus AIES, AIIDE, ICWSM, IAAI, HCOMP, EAAI, and KDD. Its awards include AAAI Fellowship, the $10,000 ACM-AAAI Allen Newell Award, the AAAI/EAAI Outstanding Educator Award created in 2016, the $1 million AAAI Squirrel AI Award for AI for the Benefit of Humanity, and Senior Member status requiring at least five consecutive years of AAAI membership and ten years of professional activity.
