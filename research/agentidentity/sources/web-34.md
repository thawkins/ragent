The OpenID Foundation released a whitepaper, “Identity Management for Agentic AI: The new frontier of authorization, authentication, and security for an AI agent world,” compiled by its Artificial Intelligence Identity Management Community Group, addressing secure authentication, authorization, governance, and accountability for AI agents. It says current standards, SSO/user-management infrastructure, and the emerging Model Context Protocol (MCP) can secure today’s relatively simple agents within single trust domains, but autonomous agents that spawn sub-agents, cross organizational boundaries, and make thousands of daily decisions will require new interoperable standards for recursive delegation, cross-domain trust, lifecycle management, and governance. The Foundation urges developers, standards organizations, and enterprises to act now to avoid fragmented proprietary solutions, and notes its OpenID Connect standard is used by billions of people across millions of applications while its Financial Grade API has become a standard for Open Banking and Open Data.
