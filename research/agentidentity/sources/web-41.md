```text
AI agent identity is an autonomous system’s unique, verifiable digital identity tied to scoped privileges, contextual awareness, and continuous lifecycle governance; because agents act at machine speed with persistent context and dynamic decision-making, they require delegation chains, contextual authorization, and behavioral validation. The page argues traditional identity models fail due to autonomy, permission complexity, and dynamic privileges, noting organizations commonly manage at least 45 machine identities per human user and that the EU AI Act’s human-oversight requirements can conflict with autonomous operation. Key risks include identity spoofing, stolen credentials, malicious inputs/hidden instructions, cascading multi-agent compromises, and accountability/audit gaps. Recommended controls include unique agent identities, least privilege, behavioral analytics, time-bound credentials, OAuth 2.0, managed identities, encryption, human-in-the-loop checkpoints, red teaming, and governance; Okta positions its platform as providing AI agent discovery, lifecycle management, and governance, with FAQs noting agents should not share identities, compromised agents can be revoked immediately, and decisions can be audited via reasoning-chain logs.
```
