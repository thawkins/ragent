```text
Agentic identity governance manages the digital identities and access rights of autonomous AI agents that can make decisions, escalate privileges, and initiate actions without human oversight—unlike traditional IAM for human and static machine accounts—because uncontrolled agents can access sensitive data, modify critical systems, or initiate unauthorized transactions, creating financial, compliance, breach, and reputational exposure. It requires five core capabilities: identity provisioning and lifecycle management, scope definition and delegation controls, real-time monitoring and behavioral analysis, audit trail generation and attribution, and incident response and revocation. The field is expected to standardize over the next two to three years, integrate with existing IAM platforms, and face more prescriptive regulation in sectors like financial services and healthcare, with cited sources including MITRE, IBM, NIST, OpenID, and Microsoft.
```
