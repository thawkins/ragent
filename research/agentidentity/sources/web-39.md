```text
AI agent identity is an autonomous system’s unique, verifiable digital identity, scoped to specific privileges, contextual awareness, and continuous lifecycle governance; unlike human identity, agents operate continuously at machine speed, make dynamic multi-step decisions, and require delegation chains, contextual authorization, and behavioral validation. Organizations already manage at least 45 machine identities per human user, and AI agents amplify non-human identity risks including identity spoofing, stolen credentials, malicious-input attacks, cascading tool/API compromises, and compliance gaps—such as the EU AI Act’s human-oversight requirements for high-risk systems. Recommended controls include identity lifecycle management, least privilege, OAuth 2.0 delegated authorization, managed identities, encryption, behavioral analytics, anomaly detection, decision-chain logging, secure delegation, Zero Trust, API-first integration, human-in-the-loop checkpoints, red teaming, and cross-functional governance. Future platforms are expected to provide unified visibility across human and AI identities, cross-organizational collaboration protocols, and quantum-resistant cryptography for long-lived agent credentials.
```
