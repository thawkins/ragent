Okta’s 2025 AI at Work report found that 91% of organizations already deploy AI agents but only 10% have a well-developed strategy or roadmap for managing non-human identities, leaving over-privileged agents as an immediate risk. Employees introduce shadow AI through unsanctioned OAuth grants—such as Cursor to GitHub, Claude to Google Workspace, and AI notetakers to calendars—and a developer-platform incident showed that a compromised third-party AI tool used pre-granted trust to reach internal systems, API keys, tokens, and environment variables. Because network and endpoint tools cannot determine which agent acted, on whose behalf, or with what scope, Okta argues identity is the control plane—85% of leaders rank IAM as critical to AI strategy—and offers Okta for AI Agents to discover, onboard, protect, and govern agents with least-privilege scoped tokens, cryptographic attribution, access reviews, audit trails, and a kill switch, while federating additively with existing IdPs via OIDC/SAML.
