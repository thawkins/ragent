Okta’s 2025 AI at Work report found that 91% of organizations already use AI agents, but only 10% have a well-developed strategy or roadmap for managing non-human identities, and 85% of leaders rank IAM as the most critical component of their AI strategy. It warns that shadow AI comes from employee OAuth grants to third-party tools—such as Cursor to GitHub, Claude to Google Workspace, and AI notetakers to calendars—creating over-privileged non-human identities; a developer-platform incident exploited an unapproved OAuth connection after the AI tool was compromised, exposing internal systems, API keys, tokens, and environment variables. Because network and endpoint tools cannot identify which agent acted, on whose behalf, or with what scope, Okta argues identity is the control plane, and its vendor-neutral Okta for AI Agents federates with existing IdPs via OIDC/SAML while providing first-class agent identity, cryptographic attribution, least-privilege tokens, integrated access reviews and audit trails, and an instant kill switch across platforms including Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, Salesforce Agentforce, Amazon Bedrock, and ServiceNow AI.
