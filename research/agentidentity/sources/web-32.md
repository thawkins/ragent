AI agent identity is a unique, cryptographically verifiable digital identity for autonomous systems, tied to scoped credentials, contextual authorization, behavioral validation, delegation chains, and lifecycle governance. The article notes organizations manage at least 45 machine identities per human user; agents differ from human/service identities through machine-speed autonomy, dynamic permissions, and chained actions, amplifying non-human identity risks such as spoofing, stolen credentials, and malicious-input compromise, while the EU AI Act’s human-oversight requirement may conflict with autonomous operation. It recommends identity lifecycle management, OAuth 2.0, managed identities, encryption, human-in-the-loop checkpoints, red teaming, cross-functional governance, and future quantum-resistant cryptography, with Okta positioning its platform for AI agent discovery, lifecycle management, and governance.
