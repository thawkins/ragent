Enterprises face a surge in human and non-human identities—application keys, devices, bots, service accounts, and AI agents—as AI and cloud apps multiply accounts, expanding attack surfaces and audit pressure under regulations including GDPR, NIS2, DORA, NIST CSF, SOX, CCPA, and the EU AI Act. The article argues legacy IGA is strained by app sprawl (72% of CIOs cited concern in a 2024 report) and immature machine IAM (Gartner’s June *Strategic Roadmap for Modern Machine IAM*), while NHIs need unique identifiers, role-based controls, lifecycle automation, recertification, cross-system integration, event-driven defenses, and AI for anomaly detection and faster decisions. It cites NIS2 becoming EU law in October 2024 and says modern IGA must combine control and adaptability, integrating with generative models and AI frameworks for secure machine-to-machine interactions.
