Salesforce describes low-code AI agent development as using visual builders, configuration tools, prebuilt AI models, and vibe coding—rather than custom coding and specialised ML engineering—to build, test, deploy, and observe autonomous agents that act across business systems under defined rules and guardrails. It claims this lowers technical barriers, enables faster iteration, broader business/IT participation, lower maintenance, and incremental deployment, while traditional AI development requires custom coding, dedicated data pipelines, and specialised ML expertise. Because agents handle sensitive data, Salesforce stresses lifecycle security and governance—data masking, role-based access controls, continuous testing, monitoring, audit logging, and policy enforcement—and cites risks including data leaks, adversarial manipulation, and bias/compliance violations such as GDPR and HIPAA. On the Agentforce 360 Platform, it offers Agentforce Builder, DevOps Centre, and AgentExchange, a marketplace of prebuilt actions, topics, and templates, to configure, deploy, and manage agents within Salesforce workflows.
