In an SC Media commentary, Roy Katmor (co-founder/CEO, Orchid Security) argues NHIs—service accounts, API keys, tokens, certificates, automation users, secrets, bots, and machine-to-machine access—are increasingly invisible to systems like EntraID, Okta, SailPoint, PAM, cloud IAM, and secrets vaults. Orchid’s *The Identity Gap: 2026 Snapshot* found invisible “identity dark matter” outweighs visible identity 57% to 43%, with 67% of non-human accounts created directly inside applications and 57% of applications bypassing centralized IdPs. The article warns agentic AI can pursue goals, find shortcuts, and exploit unmanaged local accounts, excessive privileges, hardcoded credentials, or orphaned accounts faster than humans can review, and recommends six practices: start from the application layer, map identity paths outside-in, tie NHIs to ownership/business context, understand intent/behavior, assess hygiene and toxic combinations, and operationalize guardrails for continuous identity observability and orchestration.
