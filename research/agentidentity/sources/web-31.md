Okta’s 2025 AI at Work report found that 91% of organizations already deploy AI agents, but only 10% have a well-developed strategy or roadmap for managing non-human identities, and 85% of leaders rank IAM as the most critical component of their AI strategy. The article warns that shadow AI comes from employee-level OAuth grants—such as Cursor to GitHub, Claude to Google Workspace, and AI notetakers to calendars—creating non-human identities with delegated access, often without verified owners; network and endpoint tools cannot identify which agent acted, on whose behalf, or with what scope, as shown by a developer-platform incident where a compromised third-party AI tool used a pre-granted OAuth connection to reach internal systems, API keys, tokens, and environment variables. Okta for AI Agents is presented as a vendor-neutral, additive identity layer—federating with existing IdPs via OIDC/SAML and spanning platforms like Salesforce Agentforce, Amazon Bedrock, ServiceNow AI, Azure, AWS, and Google Cloud—that discovers, onboards, protects with least-privilege single-call tokens, and governs agents via cryptographic attribution, human ownership, access reviews, audit trails, and a kill switch.
