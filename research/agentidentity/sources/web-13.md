```text
AgentBound, by Anuj Kaul and Qianlong Lan (eBay Inc.) and Pranay Gupta (Independent Researcher), is a runtime governance framework for autonomous AI agents that enforces behavioral oversight between authorization and execution by composing three independent authorities—delegated authorization, owner-signed behavioral constitutions, and site action contracts—via a conservative decision algebra. It introduces cryptographically verifiable governance receipts for replayable, non-repudiable audit, plus a standing delegation model with per-execution policy refreshing for long-running periodic agents. The paper also proposes AgentBound-Bench to measure governance enforcement accuracy, authority composition behavior, and policy provenance, with the invariant “scope permitted it, constitution stopped it, and the receipt proves it”; it explicitly excludes prompt injection, jailbreaks, model-alignment failures, and hardware side-channel exfiltration, and reports that empirical results will appear in a future release.
```
