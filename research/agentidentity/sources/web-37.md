BNB Chain has implemented ERC-8004, an identity standard that gives autonomous AI agents persistent onchain identities and verifiable reputation/history across platforms and sessions, rather than resetting in each application. BNB Chain’s low fees and fast settlement make frequent identity verification economically viable for machine-to-machine operations. The standard is positioned as foundational infrastructure for an open agent economy, enabling trust evaluation, automated payments, machine-verifiable work completion, and reputation-based selection with reduced human oversight.
