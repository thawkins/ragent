Low-code AI agent development uses visual builders, configuration tools, and prebuilt AI models to build, test, deploy, and observe autonomous agents that can act across business systems—e.g., routing service requests, escalating alerts, retrieving knowledge, or updating records—unlike traditional AI development’s custom coding, dedicated data pipelines, and specialized ML expertise. Salesforce’s Agentforce 360 Platform supports pro-code, low-code, and vibe coding with Agentforce Builder, DevOps Centre, and the AgentExchange marketplace of prebuilt actions, topics, and templates; cited benefits include faster iteration, broader participation, lower maintenance, and incremental deployment. The page stresses lifecycle governance and guardrails—data masking, role-based access, continuous testing, monitoring, and audit logging—to address risks like data leaks, adversarial manipulation, and bias/compliance violations under GDPR or HIPAA.
