```text
AI agent identity is an autonomous system’s unique, verifiable digital identity tied to scoped privileges, contextual awareness, and continuous governance; unlike human or traditional service-account identity, agents require dynamic least-privilege authorization, behavioral monitoring, delegation controls, and audit trails. The page notes organizations often manage at least 45 machine identities per human user, while risks include identity spoofing, stolen or long-lived credentials, malicious-input compromise, chained tool/API dependencies, and governance gaps such as the EU AI Act’s human-oversight requirement for high-risk systems. It recommends lifecycle management, OAuth 2.0, managed identities, encryption, human-in-the-loop checkpoints, red teaming, Zero Trust, and cross-functional governance, and says Okta offers identity solutions for AI agents with discovery, lifecycle management, and governance.
```
