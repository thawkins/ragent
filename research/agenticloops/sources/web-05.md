The page records the academic article "Sketchar: Supporting Character Design and Illustration Prototyping Using Generative AI" by Long Ling, Xinyi Chen, Ruoyu Wen, Toby Jia-Jun Li, and Ray LC, published in *Proceedings of the ACM on Human-Computer Interaction* (Vol. 8, Issue CHI PLAY, Article 337, online 15 Oct 2024; DOI 10.1145/3677102). The paper addresses communication challenges in game character design between designers (who create narrative content but have limited artistic ability) and illustrators, and introduces Sketchar, a Generative AI (GenAI) tool that lets designers prototype game characters and generate images from conceptual input, providing visual feedback that improves communication with illustrators. In a mixed-method study, reference images co-created with Sketchar fostered refinement of design details and could be incorporated into real-world workflows, and designers without artistic backgrounds found the workflow more expressive and worthwhile, demonstrating GenAI's potential to enhance interdisciplinary collaboration in the game industry. Keywords: collaboration, creativity support.
