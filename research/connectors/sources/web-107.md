The Claris Connect help page presents JSON schema examples of custom connectors for OpenAI, Google Maps, and Shopify. OpenAI uses bearer-token authorization with `{{authFields.api_key}}` and actions for `/completions` and `/edits` plus a `/models` test connection; Google Maps uses `custom_query` authorization with handlebars and geocode/directions actions; Shopify uses `custom_headers` with `X-Shopify-Access-Token` and actions across GET, POST, PUT, and DELETE for customers, products, and order transactions.
