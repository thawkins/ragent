Unstructured Transform, via claude.com/connectors/unstructured-transform, converts PDFs, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, HTML, email, scanned images, and ~70 other formats into Markdown, element-level JSON, HTML, or plain text. Its configurable pipeline partitions documents into elements (titles, paragraphs, tables, lists, images) with layout/page metadata; optionally enriches via vision-language passes (image/table descriptions, table-to-HTML, named-entity recognition, generative OCR); chunks for retrieval; generates embeddings for a vector store; and can extract JSON against a provided or drafted schema. Requests are asynchronous jobs returning results via a short-lived download link, handle up to 10 files at 50 MB each, require a free Unstructured account, and expose tools including check_job_status, get_job_results, request_file_upload_url, start_extraction_job, start_transform_job, and suggest_extraction_schema_for_file; Anthropic warns it does not control or verify third-party connector tools.
