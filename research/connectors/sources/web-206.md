```text
An AI email connector links an AI assistant like Claude or ChatGPT directly to an email platform, enabling it to draft campaigns, build list segments, and schedule sends from a single conversation rather than just generating copy. It is powered by MCP (Model Context Protocol), an open standard supported by both assistants, though users need not understand MCP to use it. Connectors do not send automatically—human approval is required—and they do not replace strategic judgment, may need voice editing, and only access data within the connected email platform unless other tools are connected. They differ from email automation, which runs trigger-based sequences, and the two are complementary.
```
