```text
The article argues that “MCP skills” are really two complementary layers: MCP, an open standard giving AI agents standardized access to tools, data, and external systems, and skills, reusable operating procedures with instructions, checks, and approval gates that make workflows repeatable. Comparing Claude and Codex through a five-skill StoriesOnBoard workflow—product-discovery, story-map-creator, story-map-gap-finder, story-map-navigator, and story-refiner—and a small “Daily Reset” product brief, it notes both use `SKILL.md` folders but differ in invocation (`/skill-name` vs `$skill-name`), locations (`.claude/skills/` vs `.agents/skills/`), distribution (Claude plugins/`.skill` uploads vs Codex plugins/local folders), and host-specific controls (Claude frontmatter/forked execution vs Codex `agents/openai.yaml` metadata). It stresses dependency detection, human approval checkpoints, read-back verification, and migration by unpacking `.skill` ZIPs into `.agents/skills/` and adjusting invocation, tool dependencies, Claude-only features, and output surfaces.
```
